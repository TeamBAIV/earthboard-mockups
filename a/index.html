LAUNCH SEQUENCE

Three, two, one, play.

Set it up once. After that, every change takes seconds.

3

Mount each pedal

Stick each pedal on a Gravity Boot, our magnetic pedal tray. Loop-side velcro is already on it.

2

Land it on the rails

Magnets grab the steel rails and 9 volts flow through the boot to the pedal. No cable to plug in.

1

Play anywhere

Run it all on a quiet battery or plug into AC. Roll a pedal off, set a new one on, done.
