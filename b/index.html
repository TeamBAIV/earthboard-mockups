Focus on the music. Not the set-up.

Earthboard is the steel-rail powered pedalboard. Pedals ride on magnetic trays and get power the second they touch the rails. Battery or wall power. No ripping velcro off the board and no power brick.

Mag-Lock

"The ripping sound of velcro is like fingernails on a chalkboard."

Each pedal sits on a magnetic tray that grabs the steel rails. Lift it off, set it back. No ripping.

Iso-Lator

"Isolating power supplies take up space and add more weight."

The ISO Boot isolates one pedal right where it sits, at 9, 12, 15, 18 or 24 volts.

Hum Killer

"Nothing worse than noisy hum from dirty power circuits."

Run the board on a rechargeable battery, away from the venue's wall power. Isolate any noisy pedal with an ISO Boot.

WALL MODE

Clean power? Plug in.

Got quiet power at home or in the studio? Plug into the Power Station and play all night. Studio owners run theirs this way.

  • 9V DC wall adapter, 3A
  • Power ports on both sides of the board
  • Stomp back to battery for the next gig
